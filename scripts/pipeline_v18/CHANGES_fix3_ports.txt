   yawed -0.75 deg (fit median 0.35 mm); the chassis scan about a plane yawed +0.5 deg. At x 300 the two symmetry planes are 6 mm apart.
   Each part's openings are centred on ITS OWN plane within 1 mm (old shell -0.9, chassis -0.6): the "4.3 mm off-centre" is a

2. DESIGN (portskit.py, hook in design_gen.py, parameters fix3_ports.json)
 * Slot: stadium 120 x 20 (was 120 x 29), z 141.7 .. 161.7 = the lens band's height and R10 stadium ends, centred on the pin axes
   (z 152.7) instead of on the old-shell boxes (z 146). Top raised 160.5 -> 161.7 for pin headroom (gap to the band paint 163.6: 1.9).
 * Web (1.5, 3 mm behind the face as before) with TWO MIRROR-IMAGE WINDOWS, each = convex hull of BOTH pins' positions (one direct, the
   other mirrored) over capture x dz along the stroke, grown by r 4 + margin 0.5 + the draft shrink to the duct end:
   |y| 3.86 .. 49.32, z 144.27 .. tunnel top 161.19; bottom corners R4.5, top corners R1.5 into the tunnel; mullion 7.72, sill 2.9,
   D-shaped end cheeks. Mirror XOR of the windows 0.000 mm2.
 * Behind each window a duct (1.5 walls, 1 deg draft = the front slider's pull +x) to 1 mm in front of the chassis ledge (x 307.7),
   its top + sides (hood) on to 1 mm in front of the chassis face (x 304.5). One end plane for both ducts; the -y duct is the exact
   mirror of the +y duct. Tunnel, web, ducts: closed solids (6 components, 0 open / 0 non-manifold / 0 unpaired edges), drawn by the
   existing PortSleeves object (arrays sleeve_v / sleeve_f; window outlines in ports_wp / ports_wm / ports_tw / ports_xw).
 * Seen from the front everything within the first 6 mm (face, tunnel, web, windows, ducts) is mirror-symmetric (port frame mirror
   deviation 0.0003 mm). What stays asymmetric is the black chassis funnel 17 mm behind the face (its mouth sits +3.8 / +1.6 mm off the
   window centres) - the real +1.95 mm chassis offset, deep and black on black.
 * Water: the slot is a closed channel from the face to the chassis, so cavity water (the deck drain at x 307 y 0 z 191.5 drops behind
   the mullion onto the chassis ledge, z 140.9) cannot reach it; window bottoms 2.9 above the tunnel floor (sill), the floor and the
   duct floors drain forward (draft), anything inside the windows goes to the chassis pockets as in the OEM design.
 * Structure: pin abuse case (pin outside the capture, 80 N = traction limit mu 0.7 x 12 kg) on the mullion: web alone 1.5 x 7.72 would
   see ~58 MPa (PC/ABS yield ~55); with the two duct walls behind it (U channel 7.72 x 6.5 deep) I = 187 mm4, sigma = 4.3 MPa.
 * Renderer: dock drawn in the seated pose (ports3.dock_dy 5.8) with clean d 7.94 pins + 15 x 18.6 collars on the fitted axes (the scanned pins are
   open underneath); bpy view_layer.update() after placing the dock so CLIP / CLIPY cut it where it is.
