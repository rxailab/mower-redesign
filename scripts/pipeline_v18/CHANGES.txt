1. WHAT CHANGED VERSUS v17 (summary of v18 -> v18c -> fix 1 -> fix 2)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
v17: a one-piece body with a snap-on top cover, gas-strut lid, scattered details.
v18 / v18c ("Orbit"):
 - A light ceramic-grey snap-on COVER over a dark graphite one-piece BODY (shell wall + integrated skirt + inner deck screwed to the
   chassis with 14 hidden screws). Real 2.5 mm walls: both parts are closed solids (shell_wall.py), every free edge rolled.
 - One joint detail everywhere: gap 0.8, cover 0.5 proud, rolled edges R0.8 (cover) / R0.5 (core).
 - Identical smoked lens bands front and rear under a level brow at z 190; flush camera / IR / LED pupil at the front, a dot at the rear.
 - A flush, same-colour lid over the STOP key and the display on a hidden virtual-pivot arc-track hinge (stop 75 deg, push-push latch,
   continuous seal, drained gutter). No gas struts. Exposed drive wheels.
fix 1 (f1): C2 roof, dense plan outline (no centre ridge), level hem, real snap legs / datum pins / seat pads / drains, heat-staked
   well, lens-groove section, controls stand-in, welded final mesh.
fix 2 (f2) - this round (finding IDs from the round-2 reviews in brackets):
 - SECTION: one G2 superellipse shoulder (a 34 x 26.5 mm corner, n 3.4, zero curvature at the wall and at the roof) of the SAME
   absolute size along the whole body and round the nose and tail, for both the pinched and the full-width surface [A1, A6, A7].
   Walls: vertical beside the wheels (171 envelope, chassis post 10), 1.5 deg tumblehome / draft on the haunch, nose and tail [R2E11].
   Crown 6 -> 3 mm (tauter roof) [A7]; haunch tuck 0.35 -> 0.2 [A3].
 - PART LINE: an explicit side-view curve (partline.py): level brows z 190, the R128 wheel orbit about the hub, the base line z 123,
   a convex R90 TAIL SWEEP tangent to the tail brow at x -262, and true-circle R20 fillets at every concave corner; the field is the
   exact signed distance to it [G4]. The tail sweep replaces the forward-leaning S-rise: no thumb, no ear flaps, the line leaves the
   brow at the same distance beyond the lens end at both ends (front 96 / rear 101 mm, measured lens tip -> first off-brow point), and
   its x(z) never increases upward, so the cover lifts straight off (0 of 337 381 cover vertices collide) [G2, A3, R2E2].
 - ZONE SINK: the 0.5 mm step trade beside the wheels is spread over |n_y| 0.15..0.995 and an 80 mm C2 ramp in x (was 0.55..0.9 and
   35 mm: an S-wave of +-2.5 1/m); the 171 limit is now measured on the FINAL outer skin (170.91) [A1 (2), F-S1].
 - Nose / tail rake back to z1 = 0.19 (no forehead lean, no concave tongue above the tail brow) [G3, A8].
 - Rear: the three decorative grooves are deleted [G1, R2E9, A4]; the lower rear half tucks in 3 mm at the hem (was flared out 3 mm) [A4].
 - Roof: ONE graphic. The white status stroke and the orange STOP stroke are gone; a flush 2K STOP pad (d 18, accent colour, white
   stop square) on the lid over the STOP key, ringed by the status halo (1.2 mm clear ring, lit from the control panel through the lid
   when closed) [A2, G5, R2E4].
 - STOP function: a cored plunger (OD 7, TPE tip) under the pad, 0.5 mm over the real STOP key when closed; the latch over-travel
   (1.5 mm at the striker) moves the tip 1.29 mm at the key, i.e. presses it 0.79 mm, then the push-push latch releases [R2E4].
 - Front: the two bare charger "nostrils" become ONE stadium service slot centred on y = 0 (120 x 29, 3.5 mm under the band), with a
   web 3 mm behind the face that carries the two (off-centre, fixed) port openings [A5, L7].
 - Nose corner floor: one closed 2.5 mm solid per side, mirror-symmetric, clipped to the bumper's own plan hull - 1 mm (never past its
   rear end or outer edge), >= 1.5 mm from the bumper, R0.5 edges, 2 mm under the nose wall and tied to its foot by ribs [F-M1, R2E7, G8, A9].
 - Cover kit rebuilt [R2E1, R2E6, R2E8]: 10 snap legs, each placed where the deck is level (<= 15 deg over -3.5..+4 mm), root fused into
   the inner skin over its whole section and necked to 1.5 mm (0.6 t) with two 1.2 mm gussets; 2 CORED datum pins (wall 1.0) with
   conformal fused tops, 0.5 deg draft, gussets; nose hole round, TAIL HOLE A 4.3 x 13.3 SLOT along x; 18 cored seat pads (OD 6, wall 1.2)
   on level deck lands (<= 5 deg) all round; stake pins r 0.75 through clearance holes in the well ears [F-N1].
 - DECK: a real 2.5 mm closed solid (441 k faces, 0 open / 0 non-manifold edges) with REAL holes: 10 leg windows 9.6 x 3.8, the
   round datum hole + the slot, 10 drain slots 6 x 3 at the deck's lowest line next to the wall, 14 screw-boss holes, 2 clearance slots
   over the chassis ribs at x 103..144; its edge runs 0.5 mm into the body wall (the joint). 0 legs / barbs / pins / pads / chassis
   points inside it [R2E5, F-S5, R2E3].
 - Lid underside ribs: two closed rings (offsets 30 / 50, R20 corners) clear of the hinge pods, diagonals and front / rear ribs mirrored [G9].
 - Controls stand-in: display window symmetric on y = 0 (+-58), window and key cap both R4, planar tops render flat (no shading
   creases) [G7, F-S2]; a dark control-tower stand-in under the panel (never floats in exploded views) [F-N2].

6.7 INTEGRATION [fix3-int] - interactions found and how they were resolved

   lid.spring_N 4.2 -> 5.2 for 30.1 g (analytic) of STOP parts. Resolution: stopkit.build now runs BEFORE lidmech.build; stopkit.mass_mesh
   takes the STOP parts from their closed meshes (density per part, minus the skin they replace: 26.0 g net, COM 155 mm (cap) .. 86 mm
   (links' rear pivot) in front of Q) into LP['extra_mass'], so lidmech's mass / gravity and the h3 spring report see ONE lid:
   166.6 g (fix-hinge 140.6), gravity torque 0.1414 N m closed / 0.0404 at 75 deg (fix-hinge 0.1092 / 0.0315); spring_N removed.
   With the fix-hinge spring this lid would NOT open (net incl. rail friction -0.008 N m closed, -0.017 at 20 deg). No spiral in the
   5.5 mm wide r 5 drum reaches the fix-hinge margin within its limits (<= 897 MPa, stress ratio >= 0.455, fill <= 0.62, <= 45 % of the
   available turns: best +0.0116 even at r 5.5); a r 6 drum does not fit the pod (flange 1.2 into the pocket). Chosen: drum 5.5 -> 7.0
